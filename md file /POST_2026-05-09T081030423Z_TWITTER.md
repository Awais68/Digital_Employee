PAKISTAN FUEL CRISIS: Petrol crosses Rs400/litre — Here is Why.

Pakistan is facing its worst fuel price shock in 50 years as petrol hits Rs458/litre and diesel soars to Rs520/litre. Here are the key reasons:

1. Strait of Hormuz Blockade — Iran closed the strait after US-Israel attacks, disrupting 12M barrels/day (11.5% of global supply).

2. Oil Import Bill Exploded — From $300M to $800M, erasing 2 years of economic progress.

3. IMF Pressure — Pakistan forced to raise petroleum levy to record Rs160/litre under IMF program constraints.

4. Inflation Surge — CPI inflation hit 10.9% in April, SBP raised rates to 11.5%.

5. Global Oil Spike — Brent crude at $115/barrel, diesel over $250/barrel.

The burden falls heaviest on common citizens — transport fares, food prices, and daily essentials are all skyrocketing.
