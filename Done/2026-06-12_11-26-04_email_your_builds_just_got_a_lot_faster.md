Your builds just got a lot faster

Lovable now spins up subagents in parallel to research, review, and build at the same time. On complex prompts, multiple agents divide the work — so instead of waiting for one long sequential run, you get results faster, with each subagent focused on its part of the job. You can see every step in the activity log, so nothing is a black box. Trigger it automatically on complex builds, or ask for it explicitly.
